Guest House Near ISKCON Temple Vrindavan

ISKCON Seva Sadan is situated in Madhuvan Colony, Raman Reti, Vrindavan — just 450 meters (a flat 5-to-7 minute walk) from Sri Sri Krishna Balaram Mandir. Review verified walking and driving distances, transit connections, and route guidance below.

Verified Distances to Major Landmarks & Temples

1. ISKCON Seva Sadan to ISKCON Temple (Walking Distance)

Our property is positioned in Madhuvan Colony, a quiet, peaceful residential enclave in Raman Reti. To reach Sri Sri Krishna Balaram Mandir, guests simply take a paved, tranquil street walk of 450 meters.

  • Morning Mangala Aarti (4:30 AM): You can walk comfortably to the temple gates in under 6 minutes without relying on night-time auto-rickshaws or cabs.
  • Srila Prabhupada Samadhi: Direct pedestrian access right opposite the temple courtyard.
  • Govinda's Restaurant & MVT Bakery: Pure vegetarian sattvic dining options situated right along the 5-minute walking path.

2. Guest House Near Prem Mandir (1.8 km)

Prem Mandir, famous for its carved white marble craftsmanship and evening illuminated fountain displays, is situated merely 1.8 kilometers away.

  • Travel Time: 5 to 7 minutes via electric rickshaw (available right outside ISKCON Seva Sadan).
  • Fare: Typical sharing e-rickshaw fare is ₹10 to ₹20 per passenger; private hire is ₹50 to ₹70.
  • Tip for Darshan: Visit around 6:30 PM to witness both the evening aarti and the dramatic exterior light animations.

3. Guest House Near Banke Bihari Temple (3.2 km)

Sri Banke Bihari Mandir is located in old Vrindavan, approximately 3.2 kilometers from our guest house.

  • Why Stay in Raman Reti: Old Vrindavan narrow lanes around Banke Bihari Temple are heavily congested, pedestrian-only, and noisy. Staying at ISKCON Seva Sadan gives you quiet sleep, clean AC rooms, and peaceful ashram vibes while keeping Banke Bihari just 12 minutes away by e-rickshaw.
  • Drop-off Point: E-rickshaws drop visitors at VIP Road or Vidyapeeth चौराहा, from where the temple entrance is an easy 3-minute walk.

4. How to Reach ISKCON Seva Sadan

Vrindavan is easily accessible from Delhi NCR, Agra, Jaipur, and all major Indian cities via road and railway networks.

From Mathura Junction (13 km)

Mathura Junction is connected to Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, and Jaipur with hundreds of trains daily. From Mathura Junction, take a pre-paid auto or cab directly to ISKCON Temple / Madhuvan Colony, Vrindavan (30–35 minutes, approx ₹250–₹350).

From Vrindavan Railway Station (3.5 km)

Serviced by local DMU and shuttle trains from Mathura. E-rickshaws take 10 to 12 minutes to reach Madhuvan Colony.

By Road via Yamuna Expressway

From Delhi NCR / Noida / Gurgaon: Drive down the 6-lane Yamuna Expressway (approx 145 km / 2.5 hours). Take the Vrindavan Cut exit and head straight to Bhaktivedanta Swami Marg.

Nearest Airports

Indira Gandhi International Airport, New Delhi (DEL) is approx 165 km away (3-hour drive). Kheria Civil Airport Agra (AGR) is approx 70 km away (1.5-hour drive).
